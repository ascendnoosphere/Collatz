\begin{abstract}
For odd multiplier and odd offset the raw division-first map on the
2-adic integers is conjugate to the golden-mean shift. We construct the
inverse itinerary map, calculate every finite cylinder, and derive its
invariant mixing probability, entropy, periodic-point counts, and zeta
function. The first-division map has a different clock and a full binary
coding. Even multipliers with odd offset instead give two fixed points,
zero topological entropy, and an explicit classification of invariant
probabilities. These are statements about compact 2-adic dynamics,
separate from integer capture or Archimedean escape.
\end{abstract}

\section{Domain and continuity}

Let \(\Z_2\) be the compact ring of 2-adic integers, with metric
\(|x-y|_2\), and let \(\mu\) be its normalized additive Haar probability.
For \(q,c\in\Z_2\), define
\[
T(x)=\begin{cases}x/2,&x\in2\Z_2,\\qx+c,&x\notin2\Z_2.\end{cases}
\]
The two domains are clopen. Division by two is continuous on
\(2\Z_2\); multiplication and addition are continuous. Consequently
\(T\) is a continuous self-map of \(\Z_2\), including when its
coefficients are ordinary signed integers.
All logarithms below are natural.

\section{Odd coefficients and legal raw itineraries}

Assume throughout this section that \(q,c\) are odd, meaning that both
are units modulo two. Define the parity itinerary
\[
\Psi(x)_j=T^j(x)\bmod2\in\{0,1\},\qquad j\ge0.
\]
An odd state maps to an even state. Thus every itinerary belongs to
\[
\Sigma=\{s\in\{0,1\}^{\N}:s_j s_{j+1}=0\text{ for all }j\},
\]
the one-sided golden-mean shift. Write \(\sigma\) for left shift.

% Inverse cylinders preserve terminal parity, which is needed for the final affine guard.
\begin{theorem}[Constructive raw-clock conjugacy]
\label{thm:conjugacy}
The map \(\Psi:\Z_2\to\Sigma\) is a homeomorphism and
\(\Psi T=\sigma\Psi\). For a legal word \(w\) of length \(h\ge1\),
let \(a(w)\) count its zeros and let \(\delta(w)\) be one if its last
letter is one, and zero otherwise. Its source cylinder \(C_w\) is a
single 2-adic residue class of modulus \(2^{a(w)+\delta(w)}\), and
\[
\mu(C_w)=2^{-a(w)-\delta(w)}.
\]
Every depth-\(h\) cylinder has diameter at most
\(2^{-\lfloor h/2\rfloor}\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The inverse branch functions are
\[
L_0(y)=2y,\qquad L_1(y)=(y-c)/q.
\]
The first is defined on all of \(\Z_2\) and yields an even predecessor.
The second is used on even \(y\) and yields an odd predecessor, since
\(q,c\) are odd. It is an isometry; \(L_0\) contracts distances by two.
For \(w=w_0\cdots w_{h-1}\), put
\[
E_w=\begin{cases}2\Z_2,&w_{h-1}=1,\\\Z_2,&w_{h-1}=0,\end{cases}
\qquad
C_w=L_{w_0}\circ\cdots\circ L_{w_{h-1}}(E_w).
\]
If a nonfinal letter is one, its next letter is zero, and \(L_0\)
ensures that its inverse input is even. The final constraint \(E_w\)
ensures the same guard at a final one. Consequently every application
is legal, and the resulting set is exactly the source cylinder.
The affine composition has slope \(2^{a(w)}q^{-b(w)}\), where \(b(w)\)
counts ones and \(q^{-b(w)}\) is a unit. Its image of \(E_w\) is
therefore a coset of \(2^{a(w)+\delta(w)}\Z_2\), proving nonemptiness,
the cylinder description, and its Haar measure.

The cylinders for prefixes of any \(s\in\Sigma\) are nonempty nested
compact sets. A legal word has at least \(\lfloor h/2\rfloor\) zeros,
so their diameters tend to zero. Compactness gives an intersection and
the diameter bound makes it a singleton. This constructs the inverse
of \(\Psi\). Every finite itinerary condition is clopen, hence
\(\Psi\) is continuous. A continuous bijection from the compact
space \(\Z_2\) to the Hausdorff space \(\Sigma\) is a homeomorphism:
images of closed sets are compact and hence closed. Finally, deleting
the first itinerary letter is exactly one raw application of \(T\).
\end{proof}

For instance, the Haar measures of the cylinders for
\(0,1,00,01,10\) are respectively \(1/2,1/2,1/4,1/4,1/2\).
The word \(10\) and its first-letter
prefix have the same cylinder, because an odd event forces a division.

% Counting legal words determines entropy for a generating sequence of clopen partitions.
\begin{corollary}[Entropy and periodic points]
\label{cor:entropy}
Put \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\). The raw entropy is
\[
h_{\mathrm{top}}(T)=\log\varphi.
\]
For every \(h\ge1\), the number of 2-adic points fixed by \(T^h\) is
\[
\#\operatorname{Fix}(T^h)=\operatorname{tr}(A^h),\qquad
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
In particular the counts at times \(1,2,3,4,5\) are \(1,3,4,7,11\).
The Artin--Mazur zeta function on \(\Z_2\), as a formal power series, is
\[
\exp\left(\sum_{h\ge1}\frac{\#\operatorname{Fix}(T^h)}h z^h\right)
=\frac1{1-z-z^2}.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The number \(W_h\) of legal words satisfies
\(W_0=1,W_1=2,W_h=W_{h-1}+W_{h-2}\), so \(W_h=F_{h+2}\).
Its exponential growth rate is \(\varphi\), by the two roots
\(\varphi\) and \(-1/\varphi\) of the recurrence.
The parity cover joined over \(h\) raw steps has exactly \(W_h\)
nonempty disjoint atoms. The shrinking-cylinder bound shows that every
finite open cover is refined by a sufficiently long cylinder partition.
Its successive joins are then refined by partitions whose lengths
differ by a fixed constant. Their exponential growth rates coincide,
proving the asserted topological entropy from the open-cover definition.

A sequence fixed by \(\sigma^h\) is uniquely its length-\(h\) word,
repeated forever. That word must also obey the last-to-first guard.
Such cyclically legal words are the closed walks of length \(h\)
in the two-vertex graph with adjacency matrix \(A\), counted with
their specified initial vertex by \(\operatorname{tr}(A^h)\).
The conjugacy proves the periodic-point formula, including points
whose primitive period divides \(h\). The eigenvalues of \(A\)
are \(\varphi,-1/\varphi\), so the formal identity
\(\sum_{h\ge1}\lambda^h z^h/h=-\log(1-\lambda z)\) gives
\(\det(I-zA)^{-1}=(1-z-z^2)^{-1}\).
\end{proof}

\section{An invariant mixing probability}

% Stationary density is computed using both inverse branches and their different clocks.
\begin{theorem}[Invariant mixing law]
\label{thm:measure}
The probability \(\nu\) defined by
\[
\frac{d\nu}{d\mu}(x)=
\begin{cases}4/3,&x\text{ even},\\2/3,&x\text{ odd}\end{cases}
\]
is \(T\)-invariant and mixing. Under its itinerary coding the process
is the stationary Markov chain with
\[
\pi=(2/3,1/3),\qquad
P=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&1/2\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its parity-block Shannon entropy rate is \((2/3)\log2\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The density integrates to one. For any Borel set \(B\), the division
preimage \(2B\) has Haar measure \(\mu(B)/2\). The affine preimage
in the odd branch has Haar measure \(\mu(B\cap2\Z_2)\), since
multiplication by the odd unit \(q\) and translation preserve Haar
measure and biject that branch onto \(2\Z_2\). Thus
\[
\nu(T^{-1}B)=\frac23\mu(B)+\frac23\mu(B\cap2\Z_2)=\nu(B).
\]

For a legal word, Theorem~\ref{thm:conjugacy} gives its Haar measure.
The identity
\(a(w)+\delta(w)=1+\#\{j<h-1:w_j=0\}\) then gives
\[
\nu(C_w)=\pi_{w_0}\prod_{j=0}^{h-2}P_{w_j,w_{j+1}}.
\]
This proves the entire stationary Markov law, not just its marginals.
Let \(\Pi\) have both rows equal to \(\pi\). Direct multiplication
shows \(P=\Pi-\tfrac12(I-\Pi)\), \(\Pi^2=\Pi\), and hence
\[
P^r=\Pi+(-1/2)^r(I-\Pi),\qquad r\ge0.
\]
The joint probability of two separated finite cylinders therefore
converges to the product, with error bounded by a constant times
\(2^{-r}\) for their separation \(r\). Finite unions of cylinders
approximate every Borel set in \(\nu\)-measure: the class of sets
approximable in symmetric difference by this algebra is a sigma-algebra
and contains the cylinders, which generate the topology. Approximate
two arbitrary Borel sets first, use invariance to control the shifted
approximation error, and then take separation to infinity. This proves
mixing. Finally the chain rule gives, for \(h\ge1\),
\[
H_\nu(w_0,\ldots,w_{h-1})=H(\pi)+(h-1)\frac23\log2.
\]
The odd row is deterministic and the even row has two equal choices;
dividing by \(h\) proves the entropy-rate formula.
\end{proof}

Every invariant Borel probability for \(T\) corresponds, by \(\Psi\),
to a shift-invariant Borel probability on \(\Sigma\), and conversely.
This includes the Dirac mass at zero and the uniform masses on the
finite cycles. An invariant mixing probability need not be the only
invariant probability.

% The Perron weights give a sharp entropy bound and an equality certificate.
\begin{theorem}[Maximal entropy]
\label{thm:maximal}
The unique invariant probability of maximal metric entropy has itinerary
transition matrix and stationary distribution
\[
P_*=
\begin{pmatrix}\varphi^{-1}&\varphi^{-2}\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\pi_*=\left(\frac{\varphi^2}{\varphi^2+1},
\frac1{\varphi^2+1}\right).
\]
Its entropy is \(\log\varphi\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Take the positive vector \(v=(\varphi,1)\), so \(Av=\varphi v\),
and put \((P_*)_{ij}=A_{ij}v_j/(\varphi v_i)\).
This gives the displayed stochastic matrix; solving \(\pi_*P_*=\pi_*\)
and normalizing gives the displayed distribution.
For any stationary process on \(\Sigma\), let \(r_i\) be its
conditional distribution of the next letter given the current letter
\(i\), ignoring rows of zero stationary mass. The nonnegativity of
relative entropy gives
\[
H(X_1\mid X_0)\le
\sum_{i,j}\Pr(X_0=i,X_1=j)(-\log(P_*)_{ij})
=\log\varphi.
\]
Only allowed edges occur. On them,
\(-\log(P_*)_{ij}=\log\varphi+\log v_i-\log v_j\),
and stationarity cancels the last two expected values.
Relative entropy is nonnegative by \(\log u\le u-1\), and equality
requires \(r_i=(P_*)_{i\cdot}\) in every positive-mass row.
The entropy rate is at most \(H(X_1\mid X_0)\), since conditioning
on more past letters cannot increase entropy. If the rate is
\(\log\varphi\), every finite conditional entropy must equal this
one-step conditional entropy. Its difference is an averaged relative
entropy; its vanishing forces all finite conditional laws to depend
only on the last letter. Thus the process is Markov with transition
matrix \(P_*\). Its stationary distribution is uniquely \(\pi_*\),
and its finite cylinder probabilities are uniquely determined.
Conversely that Markov law attains the bound by the same calculation.

For completeness, the parity-block entropy rate is the full metric
entropy here. The parity partition is a finite generator by the
shrinking-cylinder theorem. If \(\mathcal R\) is any finite measurable
partition, approximate its atoms by unions of depth-\(k\) cylinders.
The probability of a mismatched atom can be made arbitrarily small;
its conditional entropy is at most the binary entropy of that mismatch
plus its probability times the logarithm of the number of atoms.
The chain rule bounds the entropy rate of \(\mathcal R\) by the
cylinder-partition rate plus this conditional entropy. Letting the
mismatch tend to zero proves the generator assertion. Transport by
\(\Psi\) completes the theorem for \(T\) itself.
\end{proof}

\section{The first-division clock}

For odd \(q,c\), define instead
\[
H(x)=\begin{cases}x/2,&x\text{ even},\\(qx+c)/2,&x\text{ odd}.\end{cases}
\]
One application of \(H\) takes one raw tick at an even source and two
raw ticks at an odd source. Its inverse branches
\(y\mapsto2y\) and \(y\mapsto(2y-c)/q\) both contract by two and
are defined for every \(y\in\Z_2\). The same nested-cylinder argument
therefore conjugates \(H\) to the full binary shift. Each depth-\(h\)
cylinder has Haar measure \(2^{-h}\), so \(H\) preserves Haar and
its marks are independent fair bits under Haar. For the raw clock,
a one must be followed by zero; these inserted division ticks explain
the stationary frequencies \(2/3,1/3\) and the golden-mean word count.

\section{Even multipliers and odd offsets}

% The fixed-set classification uses convergence for every point, not a finite sample.
\begin{theorem}[Contractive odd branch]
\label{thm:even}
Let \(q\in2\Z_2\) and let \(c\) be odd. Put
\(a=-c/(q-1)\), an odd 2-adic integer. Every nonzero seed converges
to \(a\); zero is fixed. The invariant Borel probabilities are exactly
\[
\lambda\delta_0+(1-\lambda)\delta_a,\qquad 0\le\lambda\le1.
\]
The topological entropy is zero, and every invariant probability has
metric entropy zero. The only periodic points are the two fixed points
\(0,a\). Consequently \(\#\operatorname{Fix}(T^h)=2\) for every
\(h\ge1\), and the 2-adic Artin--Mazur zeta function is
\((1-z)^{-2}\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Every nonzero \(x\in\Z_2\) has finite valuation and reaches an odd
anchor \(u\) after that many divisions. Since \(q\) is even and
\(c\) odd, the odd branch stays odd. Its exact affine formula is
\(T^j(u)=a+q^j(u-a)\). As \(|q|_2\le1/2\), it converges to \(a\),
including the constant case \(q=0\). A finite periodic orbit with a
limit must be constant: each of its finitely many states occurs along
a subsequence and therefore equals that limit. Every nonzero periodic
point is thus \(a\), and zero is fixed. Both are distinct because
\(a\) is odd. The periodic counts are two, and the formal identity
\(\exp(\sum_{h\ge1}2z^h/h)=(1-z)^{-2}\) gives the zeta function.

Let \(K=\{0,a\}\) and let \(f(x)\) be the metric distance from \(x\)
to \(K\). This is bounded, continuous, nonnegative, and tends to zero
along every orbit. For an invariant probability \(\eta\), invariance
and bounded convergence give
\(\int f\,d\eta=\int f\circ T^j\,d\eta\to0\).
It follows that \(\eta(K)=1\): each set \(\{f\ge1/r\}\) has measure
zero, and their union is the complement of \(K\). Both points are
fixed, so every and only the displayed mixtures are invariant. Their
metric entropy is zero, since every measurable partition has constant
names at these fixed points.

To bound topological entropy, fix the partition into residue classes
modulo \(2^k\). A seed whose first odd state occurs at time \(r<N\)
has all of its first \(N\) residue names determined by \(r\) and its
odd anchor modulo \(2^k\). The prior states are dyadic multiples of
that anchor, and the subsequent affine branch never enlarges 2-adic
distances. This gives at most \(N2^{k-1}\) names. Seeds divisible by
\(2^N\) have names determined by their quotient modulo \(2^k\),
giving at most \(2^k\) further names. Thus the count is at most
\((N+1)2^k\), which has zero exponential rate. Residue partitions
refine every finite open cover for sufficiently large \(k\), proving
zero topological entropy.
\end{proof}

\section{Scope and exact finite checks}

All topological, invariant-measure, mixing, entropy, and 2-adic zeta
statements in this article have written proofs, not Lean declarations.
The exact finite implementation \cite{DyadicCode26} checks legal raw
word counts and cylinder sizes on declared signed integer parameter
ranges. A periodic 2-adic point need not be an ordinary integer.
Neither the shift conjugacy nor its rational zeta function settles
integer cycle finiteness, natural-density capture, or real-magnitude
escape for odd multipliers.
